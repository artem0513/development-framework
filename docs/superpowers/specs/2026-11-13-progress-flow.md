# Progress-based managed workflow

The owner approved implementation after discussing the flow and review findings. Clear assignments authorize immediate execution within their stated scope; an internal proposal/authorization record remains for traceability without another confirmation. Unresolved business decisions and serious architectural changes go to the owner with a short evidence/recommendation/question. Routine implementation choices follow project rules and suitable colleague examples. The explicit no-example opt-out remains. Jira/wiki-specific additions were withdrawn and must not return.

Discovery checks the assignment, restores fresh relevant project/meeting knowledge, inspects relevant authorized code/history/logs/read-only data/dependencies, then checks understanding again. Hypotheses about intent are distinct from recommended solutions. Continue useful discriminating research while it changes understanding; remove the default one-pass/one-follow-up cap. Stop when sufficiently supported, no useful next check remains, required access is unavailable, an owner-only decision remains, or the owner requests a stop. Optional missing sources do not block independent approved work.

New recovery policy uses three unsuccessful cycles without verified progress as a reassessment threshold, not a universal task-attempt ceiling. Confirmed progress means newly satisfied original obligations or verified elimination of a failure, not edited bytes or new prose. The history is shared across resume, delegates and the same underlying obligation after renaming/decomposition. After reassessment a supported corrective probe can continue; lack of progress after that probe stops automatic mutation. Existing explicit hard limits and legacy saved policies stay respected. Temporary service retries use bounded provider/client policies; external mutations require reconciliation rather than blind replay.

External observations retain UNKNOWN after an attempted operation if nonvisibility does not authoritatively establish nonapplication. Only proven application or authoritative terminal absence can resolve that uncertainty. No new cloud provider, SDK, MCP or Mastra dependency is introduced.

New discovery gaps may be scoped to explicit known stages and their dependents. Unchanged independently approved stages retain authorization; absent or ambiguous impact mapping remains conservative. Final completion still requires all mandatory obligations, real checks and separate independent acceptance/review on current artifacts.

The runtime remains Python3.11+ standard library. Project-local isolation, credential boundaries, three-target packaging, dated project memory/journal, source fingerprints, explicit external authority and strict evidence gates remain. Live Codex verification is required; live Claude/Copilot and real cloud mutations are excluded by the owner's prior preference.
